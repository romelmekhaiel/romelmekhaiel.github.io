ChessQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

ChessQuest is made by Romel Mekhaiel, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the ChessQuest app. We do not run any servers, and we do not collect or store your personal information ourselves.

No ChessQuest account

You never sign up for or log in to a ChessQuest account.

Online play (optional)

If you choose to play friends online, ChessQuest uses Apple Game Center. Apple handles sign-in, matchmaking, invitations and turn notifications. Your Game Center nickname and the moves of each online game are shared through Apple with the other players in that game. We never receive this information. Apple's handling of Game Center data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the app connects to it when it opens so it can receive invitations, as all Game Center games do. If you don't play online, no game moves are shared.

Your progress stays on your device

Lesson stars, XP, streaks, your rating, saved mistake positions, achievements and settings are saved locally on your device using standard iOS storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising

The free version of ChessQuest shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), an approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also turn off tracking in the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Remove Ads purchase

The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off all ads and stops ad-related data collection. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Internet use

Lessons, puzzles and games against the computer work offline. Only ads, App Store purchases and online games use the network.

Checkmate videos

When you use "Share this checkmate", the video is made on your device. It only goes where you choose to share it.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

ChessQuest is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. Because the free version shows third-party ads, we do not knowingly allow children under 13 to use it. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, a parent can delete it from the device, which removes all of its data.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Reset all progress, or simply delete the app. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions? Email chessquest.support@gmail.com.
